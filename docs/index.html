Chain enough steps and good per-step accuracy becomes a failing product. Drag the sliders. Companion to Why Your AI Agent Will Fail by Satsawat Natakarnkitkul · AI in Practice newsletter

Mitigation semantics: none, failures go undetected · verify, an LLM verifier (+1 call/step) catches failures at the recall you set and a caught failure gets one corrected attempt · retry, code-detectable failures (schema, tool errors) get one free retry. The verify curve assumes a perfect-precision verifier; real ones false-flag. Source & notebook: github.com/netsatsawat/agent-failure-lab
